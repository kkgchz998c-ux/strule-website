About

Independent and founder-led

Strule Automation is founded and run by Jonathan Gilmour, a time-served electrician with over 15 years in the trade. He served his apprenticeship in Northern Ireland, worked as an electrical foreman, and has spent his career hands-on in demanding industrial environments — organics recycling, waste, quarrying and mining. The firm takes its name from the River Strule, which runs through Omagh in County Tyrone.

The work is straightforward: understand the site, fix the real problem, and stand behind it. Independent and vendor-neutral, honest scope and pricing, and safety that is never traded for a deadline.
