Strule Current · remote monitoring & asset tracking

See your equipment, wherever it runs.

Strule Current puts every machine's live readings, run-hours and full service history in one secure portal — so problems surface early, planned maintenance actually gets planned, and nothing important lives only in someone's head.

What you get

Your plant, online

Not another dashboard to babysit — a straightforward record of what your equipment is doing and what it needs.

Catch it early

Live readings & alerts

Run-hours, temperature, current, throughput — trended over time, with a threshold alert the moment a reading climbs out of range or a fault keeps repeating.

One place

Every asset tracked

An equipment register with run-hours, costs, maintenance and drawings for each machine — so history isn't lost in a filing cabinet or one person's memory.

Plan, don't firefight

Maintenance on time

Service driven by real run-hours and condition, not guesswork — fewer surprise breakdowns and a clear paper trail for every visit.

How it works

Fits the machine you already have

We don't need you to rip anything out. We read the data that's already there — or add a small sensor where there isn't — and get it to your portal however the site allows.

Step one

We survey the machine

Find what it can tell us — the PLC, a drive, an energy meter, or a sensor we add — and agree the handful of signals worth watching.

Step two

We fit a small gateway

A compact, industrial unit reads the machine and sends its data securely. Read-only — it never interferes with your control system.

Step three

It appears in your portal

Live readings, run-hours and history, on any device. We can watch it with you and flag issues before they cost you a shift.

Works anywhere

Even where there's no signal — or the machine moves

Connection is chosen to suit the site, not the other way round.

Choose the link that fits
Wired to your network Site WiFi Cellular (4G / LTE) Satellite — remote sites Mobile plant & location

A fixed line on a networked site, cellular where it's simplest, or satellite for a remote quarry, pump station or landfill with no coverage at all. Mobile kit — crushers, screeners, generators — is tracked by location as well as condition. If it runs, we can get it online.

Who it's for

Most useful when downtime hurts

  • Critical machines a stoppage would cost you dearly
  • Remote or unmanned sites you can't watch in person
  • Mobile and hired plant spread across locations
  • Operations running several sites from one office
Strule Current pairs naturally with an ongoing support agreement — we watch the equipment with you, and many issues get caught and sorted before they ever stop the line. You keep ownership of your data, and you decide who sees it.
