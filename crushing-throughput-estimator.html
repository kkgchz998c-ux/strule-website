Crushing Plant Throughput & Utilization Estimator

Put in a few numbers off your control screens and belt scale, and this works out where a crushing circuit is actually losing its shift: standing still, or running slow. All the math is shown, and nothing leaves your browser.

Tons per hour (TPH) the crusher is rated for on the equipment plate or data sheet.

Hours the circuit was genuinely making product, not blocked, waiting on feed, or down.
