Manufacturing — control systems

Automated cells, machines and test rigs kept running and documented.

Discrete manufacturing depends on cells, robots and machines staying up, with changes that are documented and repeatable. As an independent integrator we're not tied to one supplier — useful when your floor mixes brands. We get the cell running, then integrate and document it properly so it's repeatable and maintainable.

  • Robots & automated cells
  • Vision & test systems
  • VFDs & motion
  • Line integration & data
  • HMI / SCADA
Typical problems we fix
  • Stalled cells idling skilled labour
  • Mixed-vendor integration gaps
  • Undocumented or one-off logic
  • Poor production visibility
  • Ageing machine controls
